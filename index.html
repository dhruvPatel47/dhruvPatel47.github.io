WHO / WHAT / WHERE

I moved from monitoring other people's infrastructure to designing my own.

I started in a SOC, reading log data across multi-source enterprise environments and writing the correlation rules that decide what a human actually looks at. Then two years as an L1 support engineer, first contact when SSO broke at nine in the morning — where you learn that most outages are configuration, not code.

Now I build on AWS. My capstone was a full serverless e-commerce platform: a custom VPC across two availability zones, Multi-AZ RDS with automatic failover, eleven Lambda functions behind API Gateway, Cognito holding every credential, Stripe handling every card. Total spend across the entire build was $52.71 — cost is an architecture decision too.

Two postgraduate credentials in Ontario, one in cloud architecture and one in cybersecurity operations, on top of a computer science degree specialised in security. Based in Scarborough, looking for cloud or security work across the GTA or remote.
